Design a mobile-first responsive UI for an app/website called “BlueYellow.ca” — a platform for discovering Ukrainian-made products sold in Canada.
The design must match the structure, spacing, hierarchy, and visual style of the provided reference draft, but converted into polished product UI.
Deliverables:
– Mobile version (primary)
– Desktop version (responsive adaptation of the same layout)
🌟 GLOBAL STYLE & SYSTEM
Color palette:
– White (#FFFFFF) background
– Black (#000000) primary text
– Light grey (#EAEAEA) separators
– Accent yellow (#FFD700) for icons, stats, pin highlights
– Minimal blue accents (#1E90FF)
Typography:
– Mobile headings: 26–30px
– Desktop headings: 34–40px
– Body text: 14–16px
– Section titles: 18–20px
– Footer text: 12–14px
– Font: Inter / SF Pro / geometric sans
Spacing system (mobile-first):
– 24px section top padding
– 16px horizontal padding
– 12–16px vertical spacing between text blocks
– 24–32px spacing between major sections
Buttons:
– Height: 44–48px
– Full rounded corners (pill shape)
– Primary: solid black / white text
– Secondary: white / 1.5px black stroke
Icons:
– Line icons with yellow accents
– Size: 28–32px (mobile)
– Size: 32–36px (desktop)
📱 MOBILE-FIRST LAYOUT — EXACT STRUCTURE
1. Header (mobile)
– Left: “BlueYellow.ca” small text logo + tiny blue/yellow circular marker
– Right: 3 text links: About · FAQ · Add Product
– All in a single top bar, height ~56px
– Padding: 16px left/right
– Sticky top
2. Hero Section (mobile)
A full-width hero card with a soft vertical gradient: #FFFDEE → #EEF7FF
very faint background image (10–15% opacity).
Content (center aligned):
– Title (28–30px): Discover Ukrainian Products in Canada
– Subtitle (14–16px): 2–3 lines
– Two equal-width buttons stacked vertically with 12px gap:
• Add Product (solid black)
• Explore Map (outline)
Height: ~380–420px
Padding: 24–32px internal
3. How It Works (mobile)
Center-aligned section.
Elements:
– Section title: “How It Works”
– Subtitle: “Three simple steps to help build our community map”
– Three steps stacked vertically (NOT horizontal):
Step item layout (mobile):
– Icon (32px yellow-accent outline)
– Step title (16–18px)
– Body text (14–15px), centered
– Spacing: 16px between icon → title, 8px title → text, 24px between steps
Steps are:
Find Products
Add to the Map
Spread the Word
4. Interactive Map (mobile)
– Section title: “Explore the Map”
– Subtitle: “Interactive map showing Ukrainian products across Canada”
Map container (mobile):
– Dark background (#111)
– Rounded corners: 24px
– Height: 300–340px
– Canada-only map (exclude US + world)
– Pins MUST be small Ukrainian flags (12–16px)
– 6–8 flag pins placed across provinces
Stats row (mobile):
Displayed in three equal columns:
– Locations: 124
– Products: 347
– Stores: 89
Stats include:
– Yellow icon
– Number (18px bold)
– Label (12–14px)
5. FAQ (mobile)
Accordion list with 1px #EAEAEA dividers.
Questions:
– How do I add a product to the map?
– Is the service free?
– How do you verify products are Ukrainian-made?
– Can I suggest stores?
– Do you have a mobile app?
Chevron icon on the right.
6. Footer (mobile)
Light grey background (#F8F8F8).
Sections stacked vertically:
– About BlueYellow.ca
– Quick Links
– Support
Each with small headings (14–16px) + list links (12–14px).
Bottom note:
“Made with love for the Ukrainian community in Canada.”
🖥️ DESKTOP RESPONSIVE RULES
The desktop layout should adapt the same sections but with:
Grid:
– Max width: 1200–1280px
– Centered content
– Use a 12-column responsive grid
Hero:
– Text left-aligned
– Buttons side-by-side (not stacked)
– Increase image/gradient height
How It Works:
– Steps displayed horizontally in 3 equal-width columns
Map section:
– Map card centered
– Increase map height to 420–480px
– Stats row stays as a 3-column horizontal layout
FAQ:
– Accordion full-width but centered
– Larger gutters (32–48px)
Footer:
– 3 columns side-by-side (About / Quick Links / Support)
– Balanced spacing, 24px gutters
🎯 Goal
Generate a responsive design system where the mobile layout is the master, and desktop is a clean, expanded version of the same visual hierarchy — matching the original draft but optimized and polished.
